# Open-set test of the species model

Pre-registered in [species-open-set-prereg.md](species-open-set-prereg.md) before any photo was pulled; scored once with the shipped int8 model and the site's rule (species named at p >= 0.65). Generated by `scripts/score_open_set.py`.

**Result: FAIL.** On other plants (e = e1 + e2, 399 photos), 20.3% got a confident sundew name (95% 16.4%-24.6%, by observer), against a pre-registered bar of below 20.0%. For comparison, 77.8% of in-list validation photos get a species name at the same threshold.

## What was pulled

`scripts/acquire_open_set.py`, observation ids below 404,747,023 (queried
2026-09-30), excluding 10,123 observers, 29,804 observations and 30,042 photo
ids from every split, the transfer set and the segmentation manifests.

- **d came out short: 230 of 300** photos, 56 species. Of 2,092 candidate
  photos, 1,846 were by observers already in the corpus or transfer set
  (people who photograph unusual sundews tend to photograph listed ones too),
  and 14 hit the per-observer cap. As pre-registered, it is reported short
  rather than filled from elsewhere.
- e1: 200 selected (*Roridula* had only 22 eligible photos; the other genera
  35-36 each); one *Byblis* photo was dropped as a near duplicate (dHash)
  of another, leaving 199.
- e2: 200, from 60 random windows over all Plantae.
- No photo matched a split or transfer photo by sha256 or dHash.

## Caveats

- Research-grade iNaturalist photos only: mostly wild plants, photographed by people who post to iNaturalist. Site users may photograph cultivated plants and other things (people, objects) not tested here.
- Python ONNX Runtime on x86; the browser's WASM int8 kernels agree on about 99% of top-1 answers, so photos near 0.65 can land differently on the site.
- Photo ids and licences are in `species-open-set.json`; the photos are not in the repository.
