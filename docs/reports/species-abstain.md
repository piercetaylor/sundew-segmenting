# Abstain rule for the species model (validation)

Generated by `scripts/analyze_species_abstain.py`. Shipped model, int8 ONNX validation logits (`models/species-110/release/dinov2-s-kdw-e100-t-c576-seed17/val-logits.npz`, `scores_onnx_int8`), probabilities = softmax(logits / 0.74). 3,959 photos, 308 observers, 110 species; 23 species have under 40 training photos ("rare").

**Read this first.** Validation contains only the 110 listed species. Nothing here says how the model behaves on non-sundew photos or on *Drosera* outside the list: those may get high probabilities too. The threshold is picked on the same photos it is scored on, so the numbers are slightly optimistic.

Sanity check against `release.json`: top-1 80.0% (0.800), balanced 0.7635 (0.7635), top-5 95.7% (95.7%), section balanced 0.9097 via the species top-1 / 0.9153 via summed section probabilities (0.9097).

## Recommendation

Rule stated in the script before it was first run: the smallest top-1 probability threshold whose answered photos are at least 90% right. That is **p >= 0.65**.

- **Answer the species when p >= 0.65:** covers 77.8% of photos (bootstrap 95% 76.0%-79.7%, by observer), 90.1% of those answers right (88.5%-91.7%). Species-balanced coverage 75.2%.
- **Otherwise say "not sure" and show the top 5.** On the 878 abstained photos, the true species is in the top 5 85.8% of the time (top 3: 78.5%); the top-1 would have been right 44.6%.
- **Section fallback** (section threshold 0.7 is a judgement call from the table below, not a pre-stated rule): when the species abstains and the summed section probability is >= 0.7, name the section. That answers 48.5% of abstained photos at 95.5% section accuracy (95% 93.3%-97.4%); 11.4% of all photos get no species and no section.
- **Rare species:** coverage 68.5% at 81.9% right, against 78.6% at 90.7% for the rest. Rare species get both fewer answers and less accurate ones; the site should not imply the 90% figure holds for every species.

## Caveats

- In-list photos only; open-set behaviour is unmeasured; see `species-out-of-list-check.md` for a small spot check.
- Threshold and section threshold picked on validation; expect slightly lower numbers in use.
- Validation is photo-weighted toward common species, like real use, but real users photograph cultivated plants more than iNaturalist wild photos, and browser preprocessing differs (EXIF, resize).
- int8 results vary slightly by CPU (99.1% top-1 agreement between two server CPUs), so a photo near the threshold can flip between answer and "not sure".
