## The cost on listed species

At the same coverage, energy answers a different mix of in-list photos (the two
rules agree on answer vs "not sure" for 87.6% of validation photos), and more
of its answers are wrong:

| Rule (validation, 3,959 photos, 110 species) | Coverage | Right when answered | Balanced (answered) | Wrong answers / 100 photos |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p >= 0.65 (current) | 77.8% | 90.1% | 86.3% | 7.7 |
| energy >= 5.955 | 77.8% | 87.9% | 83.3% | 9.5 |

So energy halves confident names for other plants (21% to 11% of e) at the
cost of about 2 points of accuracy on real sundews. The addendum fixed coverage
but set no bar on in-list accuracy, so whether to switch is the owner's call,
not something this test decides. Unlisted *Drosera* are not helped (41% to 44%).

## CPU note

These logits were computed on an AMD EPYC 7713 (node c049). The primary run
([species-open-set.md](species-open-set.md), 20.3%) ran on another machine
before a session restart, and its CPU was not recorded. On this CPU the
current rule gives 21.3% on e: 11 of 629 photos cross 0.65 differently, and p
moves by up to 0.11 on these out-of-list photos, where the model is least
decided. The int8 kernels differ by CPU (as between the site's WASM and x86).
Both runs fail the bar; the comparison above uses one set of logits for every
score. The scoring scripts now record the CPU.
