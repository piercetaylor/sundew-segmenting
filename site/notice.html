About the sundew identifier

This is an identification aid, not an authority. It suggests which of 110 sundew (Drosera) species a photo shows. It only knows these 110 species. It cannot tell when a photo shows something else, like another plant or a sundew not on its list, and it may still suggest a sundew name, sometimes with high confidence. Check any identification with an expert before using it for legal, trade or conservation decisions.

How to read a result

  • Best match among 110 sundew species: the model gave one species at least 65% of the probability. In testing on photos of the listed species, about 9 in 10 of these answers were right.
  • Not sure of the species. Probably section X: no single species was likely enough, but the species of one section (a group of related sundews) together got at least 70%.
  • Not sure: neither of the above. The possible matches still include the right species most of the time, but the first one is right less than half the time.

The five most likely species are always shown with their percentages. Percentages near the 65% line can differ slightly between devices, so the same photo may get an answer on one phone and "Not sure" on another.

Species with few training photos are identified less reliably: in testing, about 8 in 10 of their confident answers were right. The full numbers are in the evaluation report. They come from photos of the 110 listed species only. How often the tool names a sundew for something else has not been measured.

For the best result, upload a clear photo of one sundew plant, taken from above or the side, with the leaves in focus.

Your photo and your privacy

Your photo is processed in your browser and is not uploaded. The model and the code that runs it are downloaded once from this site, and the identification happens on your device. The site does not read the location stored in your photo (it uses only the orientation tag, to turn the photo upright), and has no analytics or tracking. As with any website, the host (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address when you load the page.

The model

sundew-species v1.0.0 by Pierce Taylor: a DINOv2-S image classifier, distilled from a larger DINOv2-L model and trained on 23,244 iNaturalist photos of 110 Drosera species. See the model card for how it was trained and tested.
